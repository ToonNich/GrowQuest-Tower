How to use GrowQuest Tower!

GrowQuest Tower is a pixel-style hydroponic tower simulation game where you raise, monitor, and care for a living digital plant. Below is a full guide on how to play, grow, and interact with your tower plant effectively.

🧑‍🌾 1. Begin Your Journey
After logging in, you’ll land on the Home Page. Before interacting, go to the Character Page to pick your plant and give your tower a name. Each plant has a unique sprite and behavior!

🌤 2. Daily Plant Care
Caring for your plant is the heart of GrowQuest. Visit your Home page daily and:
  • 💡 Turn on/off the Grow Light to support photosynthesis
  • 💧 Water the plant to keep it hydrated
  • 🖐 Click/tap the plant to interact with it — this builds connection!
These simple actions affect how your plant feels and unlock achievements!

📆 3. Daily Check-in System
Use the Daily Check-In page to complete care-based missions like watering or using the light. Completing these missions unlocks rewards, progress stamps, and keeps your streak alive.

📷 4. Analyze and Monitor Your Plant
Want to go deeper? These tools will help you:
  • Dashboard – Real-time display of EC, pH, temp, humidity, and light
  • Camera Page – See live feed from your hydroponic tower camera
  • Plant Analysis – Upload plant images to detect diseases or nutrient issues (⚠️ This is a helper only — not 100% accurate)
  • History Page – Scroll through weekly environmental data

🏆 5. Earn Achievements
Your actions (watering, lighting, visiting pages) are tracked to unlock fun achievement badges. For example:
  • “First Light” – When you toggle the light for the first time
  • “Green Thumb” – Your first watering
  • “Curious Gardner” – Visiting this Info Page
Achievements make growing more fun and track your progress.

🔔 6. Read Notifications
Important reminders, tips, or challenges appear in the Notification Page. Tap them to view full content — it might include environmental warnings or care suggestions!

🧪 7. Be Consistent
Hydroponic plants grow based on care over time. If you neglect them, they’ll look sad (animations will reflect this). The more love you show, the more your plant will flourish!

📎 Quick Tips
  • Try changing the light color on the Dashboard (LED presets)
  • If EC or pH is off, check the tips written on the warning blackboards
  • Come back every day to keep your tower thriving

🌟 That’s it! You're ready to grow. Go water your first plant and start your hydroponic adventure now!
